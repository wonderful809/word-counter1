How to Use Remove Line Breaks

  1. Paste your text into the input text area. This works great with text copied from PDFs, emails, or web pages.
  2. Choose an option: replace line breaks with spaces to maintain word separation, or remove them entirely to join lines directly.
  3. Click "Remove Line Breaks" to process your text instantly.
  4. Copy the result using the "Copy to Clipboard" button to use your cleaned text elsewhere.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should I use "replace with spaces" vs "remove entirely"?

Use "replace with spaces" when you want to maintain word separation (recommended for most cases). Use "remove entirely" when line breaks occur in the middle of words or when you want to join lines directly without spaces.

Why does PDF text have so many line breaks?

PDFs format text for visual display, not for copying. When you copy text from a PDF, each line ends with a line break character, even if they're part of the same paragraph. Our tool removes these breaks to restore proper paragraph formatting.

Will this remove all line breaks including paragraph breaks?

Yes, this tool removes all line breaks. If you need to preserve paragraph structure while removing only single line breaks, try our Text Formatter tool which offers more advanced options.

Is my text stored or sent to a server?

No, all processing happens locally in your browser. Your text is never uploaded, stored, or transmitted to any server. Your content remains completely private.
