Character Counter

Need to track your character count? Our Character Counter is the perfect tool for social media managers, writers, and content creators who need to stay within specific character limits. Whether you're crafting the perfect tweet, writing an Instagram caption, or ensuring your text message isn't too long, this tool provides real-time character tracking with instant feedback.

This character counter goes beyond simple counting – it includes visual progress bars for popular social media platforms. The Twitter indicator shows how close you are to the 280-character limit, turning yellow as you approach the limit and red when you exceed it. The Instagram caption indicator tracks your progress toward the 2,200-character limit, helping you maximize your caption length while staying within platform constraints.

The tool displays both total characters (including spaces and punctuation) and characters without spaces. This is useful because different platforms and applications count characters differently. Some word processors don't count spaces, while social media platforms do. Our counter shows both metrics simultaneously, so you always have the information you need. Perfect for writers tracking manuscript length, students working on assignments with character limits, or anyone who needs precise text measurement.
