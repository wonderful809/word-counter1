Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between total characters and characters without spaces?

Total characters includes every character in your text, including spaces, punctuation, and line breaks. Characters without spaces excludes all whitespace, showing only letters, numbers, and symbols.

Why does Twitter have a 280 character limit?

Twitter (now X) expanded from 140 to 280 characters in 2017 to allow for more expressive tweets. Our counter helps you maximize your message while staying within this limit.

What happens when I exceed the character limit?

The progress bar will turn red to alert you when you've exceeded a platform's limit. You'll need to trim your text before posting to that social media platform.

Is my text data stored or tracked?

No, all processing happens in your browser. Your text never leaves your device, isn't sent to any server, and we don't store or track any of your content.
