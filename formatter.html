  1. Paste your text into the input text area above.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does "trim whitespace" do?

Trimming whitespace removes all spaces and tabs from the beginning and end of each line, while preserving spaces within the line. This is useful for cleaning up indented or copied text.

Can I apply multiple formatting options at once?

Yes! Check as many options as you need. The formatter will apply them in a logical order: trimming first, then removing extra spaces, then empty lines, then sorting, and finally adding line numbers.

How does the alphabetical sorting work?

The tool sorts lines alphabetically in ascending order (A to Z), ignoring case. Numbers come before letters. This is perfect for organizing lists, names, or any line-based content.

Is my text data secure?

Absolutely. All text formatting happens entirely in your browser. Your text is never uploaded to any server, stored, or transmitted anywhere. Your privacy is fully protected.
