Text Formatter

Format your text like a pro! Our Text Formatter is a comprehensive tool that offers multiple formatting options to clean up and organize your text. Whether you're a developer cleaning up code comments, a writer organizing notes, or a data analyst preparing lists, this all-in-one formatter gives you complete control over your text structure and appearance.

The formatter includes five powerful options that can be combined for maximum flexibility: Trim Whitespace removes leading and trailing spaces from the entire text and each line, giving you clean edges. Remove Extra Spaces collapses multiple consecutive spaces into a single space, perfect for text copied from various sources that may have inconsistent spacing. Remove Empty Lines eliminates blank lines, making your text more compact and organized. Add Line Numbers prefixes each line with its number, useful for references, code snippets, or numbered lists. Finally, Sort Lines Alphabetically arranges all lines in alphabetical order, ideal for organizing lists, finding duplicates, or preparing data.

You can select multiple options simultaneously to apply complex formatting in one go. For example, you might trim whitespace, remove extra spaces, and eliminate empty lines all at once to get perfectly clean text. Or you might sort lines alphabetically and then add line numbers to create an organized, numbered list. This tool is invaluable for programmers cleaning up configuration files, writers organizing research notes, students preparing bibliography entries, or anyone who needs precise control over text formatting. The flexible combination of options makes it suitable for countless use cases.
