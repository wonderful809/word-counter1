Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between Title Case and Sentence case?

Title Case capitalizes the first letter of every word, making it ideal for headlines and titles (e.g., "The Quick Brown Fox"). Sentence case only capitalizes the first letter of each sentence, like normal prose (e.g., "The quick brown fox. Another sentence here.").

What does the tOGGLE cASE option do?

Toggle case inverts the case of every character in your text. Lowercase letters become uppercase and uppercase letters become lowercase. It's useful for fixing text that was typed with CAPS LOCK accidentally enabled.

Can I convert text in multiple languages?

Yes! The case converter works with any language that has uppercase and lowercase letters, including accented characters and special alphabets.

Is my text saved or stored anywhere?

No, all text conversion happens locally in your browser. Your text is never uploaded to any server or stored anywhere. Your privacy is completely protected.
