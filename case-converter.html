Case Converter

Transform your text with one click! Our Case Converter is a versatile tool that instantly changes the capitalization of your text. Whether you accidentally typed with Caps Lock on, need to format titles properly, or want to emphasize certain text, this tool makes it effortless. It's perfect for writers, editors, students, and anyone who works with text regularly.

The Case Converter supports five different transformation modes: UPPERCASE converts all letters to capitals (perfect for headings and emphasis), lowercase makes everything small letters, Title Case capitalizes the first letter of each word (ideal for titles and headings), Sentence case capitalizes only the first letter of each sentence (standard for paragraphs), and tOGGLE cASE flips the case of every letter (useful for fixing accidentally typed text with Caps Lock on).

Using this tool is simple: paste or type your text in the input box, click the conversion button you want, and instantly see the transformed text in the output area. You can then easily copy the result to your clipboard with a single click. This is especially useful when you need to quickly reformat text copied from various sources, standardize capitalization across documents, or prepare text for different platforms with specific formatting requirements. No more manual retyping – let our Case Converter handle it for you!
