How to Use the Word Counter

  1. Type or paste your text into the text area above. The tool works with any type of text content.

  3. View detailed metrics including word count, character count (with and without spaces), sentences, paragraphs, and estimated reading time.

Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as a word?

A word is any sequence of characters separated by spaces. Numbers, URLs, and hyphenated words count as single words. Contractions like "don't" or "it's" also count as one word.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time is estimated based on an average reading speed of 200 words per minute, which is standard for adult readers. The actual time may vary based on text complexity and individual reading speed.

Is my text data private?

Yes, absolutely! All processing happens entirely in your browser. Your text never leaves your device and is not sent to any server. We don't store or track any of your content.

Can I use this for social media posts?

Yes! The character counter is perfect for ensuring your posts fit within platform limits. Twitter allows 280 characters, and we show both characters with and without spaces.
